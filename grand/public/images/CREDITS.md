## Fonts

Cairo and Manrope are self-hosted via Fontsource under SIL Open Font License 1.1. [Cairo license](../fonts/OFL-Cairo.txt), [Manrope license](../fonts/OFL-Manrope.txt). No runtime request to Google Fonts is required.
